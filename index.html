    02 / PRACTISE THE PULSE

    Rhythm exercises

    01
    Find the 1

    Listen to four steady beats. Clap gently on beat 1, then count 2, 3, 4 out loud.

    02
    Step on the beat

    March or step in place on each beat. Keep the movement small and even.

    03
    Add the “&”

    Switch to eighth notes. Say “1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &” and tap lightly on every count.

    04
    Sixteenth-note ladder

    At a slow tempo, count “1 e & a, 2 e & a, 3 e & a, 4 e & a”. Tap each syllable evenly, then try small footwork accents without rushing.

    05
    Move, then freeze

    Dance freely for four counts, then pause for four counts while keeping the beat in your head.

    Try 2–3 rounds, then take a short break. Accuracy and comfort matter more than speed.
